Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Scope. This policy covers the Quantal Health marketing website at quantal-health.com. It does not govern patient data inside a clinic's branded app. That data belongs to the clinic, lives in the clinic's own dedicated environment, and is governed by the clinic's own privacy notice — with Quantal acting as a service provider under a HIPAA-aligned data agreement. See Security & HIPAA for how that works.

Information we collect on this website

We keep this deliberately small. On the marketing website we collect:

  • Information you send us. When you use a contact or demo-request form, we collect what you provide — typically your name, clinic, email, and any message.
  • Basic usage data. Standard technical information such as your browser type, pages viewed, and general location, used to understand how the site is used and to keep it working.

We do not ask for patient health information through this website, and you should not send it to us here.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about the design partner program or a potential engagement.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising to you across other sites.

How we share it

We share website information only with service providers who help us run the site and our business (for example, hosting and email), under agreements that limit them to that purpose — and when required by law. We never trade or sell your contact details.

Patient data in the product

When a clinic runs Quantal Health, patient data is collected under the clinic's name and stays in the clinic's dedicated environment. Quantal processes it on the clinic's behalf as a service provider (a business associate, in HIPAA terms) under a signed, HIPAA-aligned data agreement. The clinic — not Quantal — is the point of contact for its patients' privacy rights.

Data retention

We keep website inquiry data for as long as needed to follow up and to meet our record-keeping obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask us what website information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product and our practices evolve. We'll change the “last updated” date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch — you'll reach the founders.
