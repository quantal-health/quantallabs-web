Daily logging

One screen, one minute. Patients tap their weight and how they're feeling — and your team sees it that morning. No menu diving, no scrolling. Designed so adherence stays high because the friction stays low.

AI Food Vision

Snap a photo of a meal. The app estimates calories and macros, so logging takes seconds instead of skipped days. For prepackaged foods or things the camera can't read, a fast searchable food database backs up the AI.

Care messaging

HIPAA-aligned, two-way messaging between patients and your care team — replacing the patchwork of texts, calls, and portal logins. Every conversation is logged, searchable, and routed to the right clinician without anyone touching a personal phone.
